A FEW THINGS BEFORE YOU JOIN US

A little helpful detail.

What time do the events begin?

Chandam Charthu begins at 6:30 PM on June 25, 2027. The wedding begins at 10:30 AM on June 26. All times are local to Kerala (IST, UTC+05:30). The time for Ottukalyanam on June 21 will be announced later.

Where are we celebrating?

On June 21, we will be at St. Sebastian’s Knanaya Catholic Church in Kandakassery, Payavoor. On June 25, we will gather at Biagio’s home in Areekara. On June 26, our wedding will take place at St. Rocky’s Church in Areekara. You can find the exact locations using the maps in the programme.

How can I confirm I’m coming?

For now, please let Biagio or Steena know directly, using the contact details you already have for us. We would love to hear that you can join us!
